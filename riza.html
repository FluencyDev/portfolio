The Challenge

New Brand in a Legacy Market

Riza Sementes was a new premium seed brand that had to earn credibility fast in a market dominated by long-standing players. My job was to make that happen.

The Thinking

Sementes means "seeds" in Portuguese, so I started there.

Rather than default to generic agribusiness cues, I leaned into the sector's own colors, orange and green, sharpened by precise geometry. My signature move: the letter I, reimagined as a seed shape, anchoring the wordmark in the product itself.
